How it works

Tap + on any day to add a task. Tap the pencil on a task to change its time, name or type, or to delete it.

In the task window, “Also copy to” repeats a task on other days, so you only type it once.

A task you have not ticked within 30 minutes after it ends is crossed out as missed. You can still tick it later. Finish all the study, class, lab and gym tasks of a day to get a celebration.

Your plan is saved only in this browser on this device. Use Data, then Download backup, to keep a copy or move it to another device.
